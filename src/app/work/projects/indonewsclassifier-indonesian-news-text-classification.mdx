## Overview

A machine learning model that classifies Indonesian-language news articles by category, wrapped in a Streamlit app so it's actually usable rather than just a notebook.

## Technologies Used

- **Python**, **scikit-learn** — text classification pipeline
- **Streamlit** — deployed, interactive demo
- **NLP preprocessing** for Bahasa Indonesia text

## Why it's featured

Out of the projects in my GitHub, this is the one that needed the least cleanup — the training methodology, the reported results, and the deployed demo all line up with each other. It's the baseline I held the other projects to.
